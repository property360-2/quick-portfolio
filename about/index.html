Specialist Profile

Turning Ideas into Automated Reality

Having spent years in labor and construction, I don't look at software as just code—I look at it as a tool that needs to be durable, functional, and built on a solid foundation. I bring that same blue-collar work ethic to software engineering. I build practical, no-nonsense custom software, workflow automation, and AI tools that solve real-world operational headaches.

Rather than just writing lines of code, I engineer complete, durable systems designed to solve operational bottlenecks and eliminate manual effort. By developing custom solutions—ranging from client management dashboards and secure customer portals to automated database integrations—I bridge the gap between complex software design and actual business ROI.

I specialize in connecting disparate tools like CRMs, Google Workspace APIs, database layers, and automation flows to create a unified, automated experience. My goal is to help businesses transition from manual spreadsheets and fragile, repetitive tasks to scalable, secure digital infrastructure.
