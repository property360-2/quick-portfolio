About Me

Beyond the Code

Hi! I’m Dionisio Alvior aka Jun, a 21-year-old full stack software developer and a 4th-year BSIS student. I love building web applications that are functional, beautiful, and user-friendly, using a wide variety of tech stacks like Python (Django + Templates), React, Next.js, Node.js (Prisma), PHP (Laravel & vanilla PHP), Supabase, and Firebase.

I’ve also embraced AI-assisted development, which helps me speed up repetitive tasks and explore creative solutions. Over the years, working with clients and receiving feedback has taught me a lot — not just about coding, but also about designing systems with better UX/UI.

Currently, I’m a Project Manager OJT at my school, leading the development of a full school portal. I enjoy collaborating with others, learning new tools, and building smarter software every day.
